I am Yingning (Ningning) Xie, a doctoral candidate at the [Edward J. Bloustein School of Planning and Public Policy](https://bloustein.rutgers.edu/) at Rutgers University. I study how transportation shapes access to healthcare and everyday wellbeing, with a focus on mobility, place, and equity.

My dissertation, *E-Cycling for Wellbeing*, examines e-bike use, access to health-related destinations, and riders' experiences through spatial analysis, a national survey, and interviews.

My research includes studies of primary care accessibility in New Jersey and shared e-bike use in New York City.
