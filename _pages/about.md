    <div class="home-intro-grid">
      <h2 id="home-intro-title">Mobility, health, and the places in between.</h2>
      <p>My dissertation, <em>E-Cycling for Wellbeing</em>, examines the opportunities e-bikes can open, how riders perceive access, and how travel fits into lived experiences of health and wellbeing. Across my research, I ask who can reach essential destinations and how the answer changes with place, resources, and mode of travel.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="home-topics">
      <div><span>01</span><h3>Access to care</h3><p>Measuring access to healthcare destinations and the qualities of the providers people can reach.</p></div>
      <div><span>02</span><h3>Travel behavior</h3><p>Studying how e-bikes and other modes are used across different places and daily needs.</p></div>
      <div><span>03</span><h3>Equity and wellbeing</h3><p>Connecting mobility with constraints, perceived access, and everyday experience.</p></div>
