  <div class="aa-intro">
    <p>This poster examines access to primary care physicians by car, transit, and e-bike across <strong>73 census tracts in Atlantic County, New Jersey</strong>, using a <strong>15-minute travel threshold</strong>. It compares access by physician attributes and examines associations with socioeconomic characteristics.</p>
    <p>The <a href="{{ '/attractive-accessibility/' | relative_url }}">journal article companion</a> presents the statewide New Jersey analysis with a 30-minute travel threshold.</p>
