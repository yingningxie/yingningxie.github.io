Time of day

For members, classic-bike trips had clear morning and evening peaks. E-bike trip counts had flatter peaks, and e-bikes reached their highest share of member trips around 3 p.m. (22%).

Infrastructure

Bike lanes, bike racks, and nearby subway stations are positively associated with both bike types. Their estimates are generally similar.

Elevation

Higher start-station elevation is negatively associated with classic-bike trips. The e-bike estimate is smaller and not statistically significant.

Neighborhood context

Daytime workers have a stronger positive association with e-bike trips. The negative association with the surrounding area's Hispanic population is smaller for e-bikes.

04 / About the study

How to read these findings

The study analyzed 3,369,117 valid trips from September 2022. It used a quarter-mile walking catchment around each station to describe nearby infrastructure, land use, population, and elevation. Negative binomial conditional autoregressive models accounted for spatial dependence among stations.

Station-area demographics describe the neighborhood around a start station. They do not identify the race or ethnicity of riders. The analysis covers one month and reports associations rather than causal effects.

Xie, Y., Smart, M. J., & Noland, R. B. (2024). “Powering bikeshare in New York City: Does the usage of e-bikes differ from regular bikes?” Transportation Planning and Technology, 47(6), 875–902. https://doi.org/10.1080/03081060.2024.2341301
