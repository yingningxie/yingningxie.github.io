projects

Projects I lead and contribute to in transportation, health, and planning.

My projects connect transportation, healthcare access, and wellbeing, alongside earlier work in participatory planning and social policy.

Project Lead

E-Cycling for Wellbeing

Doctoral dissertation · 2024–2027 · In progress

My dissertation examines e-bikes, access to health-related destinations, and wellbeing through spatial accessibility analysis, a national survey, and interviews with riders living with chronic illness, disability, or financial constraints.

The healthcare accessibility study is published in Health & Place, the survey study is under review at Travel Behaviour and Society, and the therapeutic mobility manuscript is in preparation.

Published healthcare accessibility study, with Michael J. Smart

Unmet treatment need and driving under the influence

Lead researcher · 2026–present · Under review at Journal of Transport & Health

Examining associations between unmet need for mental health treatment and driving under the influence using national U.S. survey data.

Harassment and mobility in New York City

Lead researcher · Manuscript in preparation

Investigating who reports harassment and how travel restriction, mode-related responses, and alertness vary across social groups.

Shared e-bikes in New York City

Independent study · 2023 · Published in 2024 · With Michael J. Smart and Robert B. Noland

This study compares e-bike and conventional-bicycle use in Citi Bike. Spatial statistical models examine factors associated with trip generation, including infrastructure, land use, demographics, and elevation.

Article in Transportation Planning and Technology

Studying abroad and travel behavior in Stockholm

Master’s thesis · NTNU · 2020 · Completed · Supervisor: Peter Gotsch

Using interviews and a life-course perspective, this thesis examined students’ travel experiences and attitudes while studying in Stockholm. The work also informed a public-facing article on young adults and greener mobility.
